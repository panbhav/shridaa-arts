The Artisan

Meet the Artist

Shridaa Arts is a creative space where traditional Lippan art is thoughtfully crafted into contemporary decorative pieces.

Ashima Goyal creates handmade artworks with attention to detail, combining traditional Indian craft aesthetics with designs suited to modern homes and spaces.

Originating from the rural huts of Kutch, Gujarat, Lippan Kam was traditionally made using wild clay, camel dung, and reflective glass to illuminate dark desert dwellings. Shridaa Arts breathes new life into this ancient craft using refined artistic compounds and contemporary palettes, crafting timeless art pieces that effortlessly enhance modern interiors.

The Artisanal Journey

Made by Hand. Made with Intention.

Every piece from Shridaa Arts is an original creation shaped through patience, sacred geometry, and meticulous hand-detailing.

01

Concept & Geometry

Drawing upon traditional mandalas and sacred Indian symmetry, each piece begins with careful pencil mapping of proportions.

02

Mud & Clay Relief

Specialized mud and clay dough is hand-rolled into delicate coils and sculpted directly onto wooden foundations to build textural depth.

03

Mirror (Aabhla) Work

Precision hand-cut diamond, teardrop, and convex glass mirrors are individually embedded into wet clay to capture dynamic ambient light.

04

Finishing & Sealing

Organic earth tones and metallic gold accents are brushed with care, followed by an archival protective seal to ensure decades of beauty.
